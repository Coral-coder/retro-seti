Spectrum

Hydrogen glow at each speed along the line of sight. Bumps are clouds of gas moving towards (−) or away (+) from us.

Waterfall

The last 24 hours, newest at the top. The Milky Way drifting overhead shows up as bright bands.

Sky map

Total hydrogen seen in each part of the sky you have observed. The bright band is the plane of the Milky Way.

Longitude–velocity diagram

Along the galactic plane: speed of the gas at each galactic longitude. Spiral arms appear as ridges.

Rotation curve

How fast the galaxy spins at each distance from its centre. A flat curve is the classic evidence for dark matter.

Face-on map of the Milky Way

Hydrogen placed at its distance using the rotation, seen from above. You are at the star; the centre is the cross.

What you would be doing

Everything here runs in your browser. Recordings and results never leave your computer; real observations are kept in this browser's storage so the maps keep building from day to day.
